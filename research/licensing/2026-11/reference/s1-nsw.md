## New South Wales

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority and contact | Biodiversity and Heritage Regulator (BHR), Wildlife Team, NSW DCCEEW. Phone 02 9585 6406. Email: the site hides it behind an email link, so the address is **unconfirmed**. Web: [Apply for a native animal keeper licence](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/licences-and-permits/wildlife-licences/native-animals-as-pets/apply-for-a-native-animal-keeper-licence). Online applications go through Service NSW. |
| Official keepable-species list | [NSW Native Animal Keepers' Species List](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/publications/nsw-native-animal-keepers-species-list) (OEH20160264), first published April 2016 and reprinted 9 January 2019. Requests to add species are frozen while the Independent Review of Biodiversity Legislation is under way. |
| Licence names and what each allows | **Companion Animal Keeper licence:** one animal of one species from the companion list, named when you apply. Purchase records are kept but not lodged. **Class 1 Native Animal Keeper licence:** several animals, covering more than 300 types (basic-knowledge species). **Advanced Class Native Animal Keeper licence:** an upgrade for harder species and venomous reptiles, applied for on a separate form. A licence doesn't allow commercial trade. Keepers may sell or give animals only to other licence holders, and can't dispose of an animal within 6 months of acquiring it unless they bred it. Frogs use the same licence names. |
| Exempt species | No reptiles and no native frogs. ([Frog keeper licences](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/licences-and-permits/wildlife-licences/native-animals-as-pets/frog-keeper-licences), updated 19 Jun 2026.) |
| Minimum age and guardian rules | Companion and Class 1: at least 16. For a child under 16, the parent or guardian applies in their own name. Advanced: over 18, with at least 2 years on a lower licence with related species. |
| Cost (fee year) | Companion: $50 for 5 years ($45 online, $40 pensioner). Class 1: $63 per class for 2 years, or $143 per class for 5 years. Advanced: $120 per class for 2 years, or $240 per class for 5 years. Online and pensioner discounts apply. Fees are as shown on the page updated 1 Jul 2026. Paying the fee doesn't guarantee a licence. |
| Processing time | Licences are no longer processed immediately online. An online receipt arrives within 4 hours, and the outcome takes **at least 8 weeks** because of a backlog. ([Changes to online applications](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/node/11586), updated 1 Jul 2026.) |
| Record keeping and returns | Multiple-animal licences use a record book. Record each acquisition or disposal straight away, and bred animals within 7 days. The record period ends 31 March, and the signed pages are due to the BHR by **30 April**. If you held no animals, send a nil statement. Notify the BHR within 14 days of transactions in "advanced" animals. |
| Buying, selling and moving within the state | Buy only from current licence holders, or from interstate sellers under an import licence. Keep signed, dated receipts and check the seller's licence. Advertise only in approved keeper or specialist publications, giving your licence number. Animals stay at the nominated premises, except for vet visits, sale, or up to 72 hours at a meeting or show. Notify any other move within 7 days. |
| Recent or upcoming changes | In 2025 instant online licences ended and every application is now assessed. The species list is frozen pending the biodiversity legislation review. NSW is developing a [risk-based tool](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/licences-and-permits/wildlife-licences/wildlife-licensing-reforms/risk-based-approach-for-native-animal-keeping) to decide whether each species should be licensed, covered by a code, exempt or prohibited. No dates have been published. |
| Venomous species | These need an Advanced licence in the venomous categories. You must be over 18, start at the lowest venomous category and gain at least 1 year at each level. Other requirements: lockable, escape-proof cages in a lockable room, a current first-aid certificate, a written snake emergency plan, access limited to people with the same licence, and 2 written references. ([Advanced class requirements](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/licences-and-permits/wildlife-licences/native-animals-as-pets/apply-for-a-native-animal-keeper-licence/requirements-to-qualify-for-an-advanced-class-licence), updated 1 Jul 2026.) |
| Last checked | 8 Nov 2026 |

> **Authority's internal categories (for Rowan only, never app copy):** the species list codes animals as Companion, R1 (Class 1) and R2–R5 (advanced reptiles), and frogs as A1 and A2. Venomous snakes are "Categories 1–3" within the advanced classes.

### What this means for the app
- `cost`: "basic from $63 per tier" uses a name NSW doesn't use. Change it to "Companion $50 for 5 years; Class 1 from $63 per class (2 years); Advanced from $120 per class (2 years)".
- `minage`: add that for under-16s a parent or guardian applies in their own name.
- `notes`: add the 6-month hold before you can sell or give away an animal you didn't breed, and that records are due by 30 April.
- The licence names in the grids ("Companion or Class 1 licence", "Advanced licence") and the 8-week warning are correct. No change needed there.
- The research files `full-NSW.json` and `matrix.json` use "Basic licence" for NSW's middle tier. NSW calls it a "Class 1" licence. Use "Class 1" in the next dated research folder. The shipped grids already do.
