## Victoria

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority and contact | Conservation Regulator, Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA), Wildlife Licensing. Phone 136 186. Email wildlifelicensing@deeca.vic.gov.au. Post: 475 Mickleham Road, Attwood VIC 3049. Web: [Private wildlife licences](https://www.vic.gov.au/private-wildlife-licences). |
| Official keepable-species list | Species List for a Basic Wildlife Licence and Species List for an Advanced Wildlife Licence (both PDFs, uploaded April 2021), plus *Taxa of captive-bred wildlife that may be held for private purposes without a licence* (Word file, uploaded November 2020). All three are linked from [Private wildlife licences](https://www.vic.gov.au/private-wildlife-licences). The legal schedules now sit in the Wildlife Regulations 2024. |
| Licence names and what each allows | **No licence:** species on the no-licence list, kept for non-commercial purposes. They must be captive-bred from a lawful source, not taken from the wild. **Wildlife Basic Licence:** keep, breed, buy, sell and dispose of basic-list species for non-commercial purposes. **Wildlife Advanced Licence:** the same activities, also covering advanced-list species. Basic and Advanced are 1- or 3-year licences, and all expire on 30 September. |
| Exempt species | Yes: the no-licence list. They must be captive-bred and lawfully acquired, and you should keep proof of where they came from. |
| Minimum age and guardian rules | 10. Under-18s need signed consent from a parent or guardian. On an Advanced licence, under-18s keeping venomous snakes must show documentary evidence of competency. |
| Cost (fee year) | From 1 July 2026, per year (full / concession): Basic $144.60 / $72.30; Advanced $267.60 / $133.80. Fees are pro-rata by month, and 3-year licences are discounted. |
| Processing time | Allow at least 4 weeks for assessment. After payment, the licence and record book arrive by post in about 2 weeks; they can't be emailed. Don't acquire any non-exempt animal until both have arrived. |
| Record keeping and returns | Use the hard-copy record book supplied with the licence. Record every transaction by close of business the same day, and never erase or remove pages. The annual return is due **before 14 April**. Return the book within 10 business days if you surrender the licence or don't renew. |
| Buying, selling and moving within the state | Buy only from other licence holders, and only after your licence arrives. Animals stay at the one licensed address, except for vet trips and trades with another licence holder. A carer who will hold your animals and return them within 6 months needs written approval first. Taking animals interstate needs an export permit. |
| Recent or upcoming changes | Online applications have launched, with new forms dated July 2026 and new fees from 1 July 2026. The government's [response to the Wildlife Act review](https://www.wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0039/768369/Victorian-Government-Response-to-the-Wildlife-Act-review.pdf) (September 2025) commits to the following, with no dates set: a new licence category for dangerous wildlife such as venomous snakes and large monitors; limits on breeding and collection size; moving complex-care species off the Basic licence; a fit and proper person test; and an online tool to check licences. Private crocodile keeping is being phased out, and Advanced licence holders now need written permission to acquire any crocodile ([licensing review page](https://www.vic.gov.au/wildlife-licencing-review), updated 22 Oct 2025). |
| Venomous species | Advanced licence. Under-18s need documentary evidence of competency. A separate "dangerous wildlife" licence is planned but not yet in force. |

### What this means for the app
- `licence`: "Private Wildlife Licence" is the umbrella term. The licences themselves are the "Wildlife Basic Licence" and "Wildlife Advanced Licence". The grid wording "Basic licence" / "Advanced licence" is fine.
- `notes`: add the annual return due before 14 April. Consider a line saying reforms are coming: a dangerous-wildlife licence for venomous snakes and large monitors, breeding limits, and complex-care species moving to the Advanced licence.
- Cost, age and wait are all correct. No change needed.
