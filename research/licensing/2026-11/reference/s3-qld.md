## Queensland

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority and contact | Department of the Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation (DETSI), Permits and Licensing (PAL). Phone 1300 130 372 (option 4). Email palm@detsi.qld.gov.au. Web: [Licences to keep, use or display native animals](https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/wildlife-permits/permit-types/keeping-native-animals/native-animal). |
| Official keepable-species list | [Categories of protected animals under the Nature Conservation (Animals) Regulation 2020](https://www.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0035/139697/classes-of-animals-under-the-nc-animals-regulation-2020.pdf) (undated PDF). See also [Exempt, prohibited and species class listings](https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/wildlife-permits/permit-types/keeping-native-animals/what-animals-can-i-keep/exempt-species-and-class-listings) (updated 7 Aug 2026). Any animal not on the list is prohibited. |
| Licence names and what each allows | All three run for 5 years. **Standard licence:** up to 10 easier species; no breeding, no harder species and no dangerous snakes. **Specialised licence:** up to 50 reptiles, frogs and birds, including harder species; breeding allowed within the limit; dangerous snakes (up to 50) with approval. **Advanced licence:** unlimited easier and harder species; breeding allowed; dangerous snakes up to 50 with approval, unless more are approved. On Standard and Specialised you can't sell or give away an animal within 6 months of getting it without written approval. Advanced has no such restriction. |
| Exempt species | None. QLD says "There are no exemptions for reptiles and amphibians." |
| Minimum age and guardian rules | **13.** Applicants aged 13–17 can hold a licence if they live with a legal guardian who agrees to supervise. Both names go on the licence, under-18s apply on the paper form, and applicants 18 or over apply online. A conviction for an animal welfare or conservation offence in the past 3 years rules out a new licence. |
| Cost (fee year) | 2026–27, for applying or renewing: Standard $68.20 and Specialised $365.10 (both from ABLIS, the federal business licence register, not the department). The Advanced fee is **unconfirmed**. The department's own schedule of fees PDF could not be reached. |
| Processing time | Online applications are issued immediately if no more information is needed. Otherwise allow up to 40 business days. Specialised or Advanced applications that include venomous snakes are assessed manually. |
| Record keeping and returns | Record animals in the online record book (or on approved paper forms) before you keep them, and report every transaction within 72 hours. Inspections can happen at any time. |
| Buying, selling and moving within the state | Check the other party's identity and wildlife licence, and record their licence number. This also applies to interstate trades. Moving animals into or out of QLD needs a movement advice or movement permit from the department, plus whatever the other state requires. ([Moving native and international wildlife](https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/wildlife-permits/permit-types/moving-wildlife), updated 8 Oct 2026.) |
| Recent or upcoming changes | The licence pages were refreshed on 30 Sep and 8 Oct 2026, with no change found to tiers or limits. The Nature Conservation (Single Integrated Permissions) amendment (SL 2026 No. 94, from 4 Jul 2026) doesn't affect keeping licences. Blue-tailed Monitor and Pygmy Blue-tongue are prohibited, and existing animals stay only under a non-breeding, non-trade permit. |
| Venomous species | Not on a Standard licence. On Specialised or Advanced they need approval and manual assessment, with a cap of 50 unless more are approved. |
| Last checked | 8 Nov 2026 |

> **Authority's internal categories (for Rowan only, never app copy):** Class 1 (easier, Standard and above) and Class 2 (harder, Specialised and above), set in the Nature Conservation (Animals) Regulation 2020, Schedule 3.
