## South Australia

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority and contact | Department for Environment and Water (DEW), Fauna Permits Unit. Phone (08) 8124 4972 (Mon–Fri, 10 am–4 pm). Email DEWFaunaPermitsUnit@sa.gov.au. Web: [Native animals as pets](https://environment.sa.gov.au/licences-and-permits/wildlife-permits/permit-types/keep-sell-display-native-animals/native-animals-pet). |
| Official keepable-species list | [Native animal keeping categories species list](https://cdn.environment.sa.gov.au/environment/docs/Keeping-categories-species-list.pdf) (lists unprotected, exempt and basic species; undated). The legal basis is the National Parks and Wildlife (Wildlife) Regulations 2019, Schedule 6 (version 7.3.2024). The current categories took effect on 1 July 2019. Any protected native animal not on the list is Specialist. |
| Licence names and what each allows | **No permit (exempt):** keep, sell or receive as a gift. **Basic animals:** you can keep one without a permit. Keeping two or more, or selling or giving any away, needs a **Basic permit** (Permit to Keep and Sell Protected Animals, Hobbyist/Recreational). A one-off sale or gift can use a Permit to Sell a Protected Animal instead. **Specialist permit:** needed for even one Specialist animal, such as venomous snakes or Lace Monitors. You must show the skills and facilities, and the permit also covers Basic animals. Permits run for 1, 3 or 5 years, issued by financial year. |
| Exempt species | Yes. Exempt animals must be captive-bred before you acquire them, and you should keep the receipt as proof. |
| Minimum age and guardian rules | No minimum stated. Under-18s must supply a parent or guardian declaration. |
| Cost (fee year) | 2026–27, Basic permit: $48.50 (6 months), $97 (1 year), $291 (3 years), $485 (5 years). Specialist permit: $85.50, $157, $471, $785. Permit to Sell a Protected Animal: $23.60. ([Wildlife permit fees 2026–27](https://cdn.environment.sa.gov.au/environment/docs/Wildlife-permit-fees-26-27.pdf)) |
| Processing time | Basic: 2 weeks. Specialist: 8 weeks. |
| Record keeping and returns | Keep records at the premises where the animals are held, and lodge an annual return. The due date isn't stated on the page; it's "in line with relevant regulations". |
| Buying, selling and moving within the state | Buy protected animals only from a licensed fauna dealer or a permit holder. "Sell" includes swapping and giving away. Travelling within SA with an animal needs the Unit's written permission first, and a change of address must be notified within 14 days. |
| Recent or upcoming changes | The *Biodiversity Act 2025* passed in June 2025 and is being phased in "in stages over coming years". The first draft regulations, for statutory bodies, were out for comment until 15 Sep 2026 ([DEW news, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.environment.sa.gov.au/news-hub/news/articles/2026/08/south-australians-invited-to-help-shape-landmark-biodiversity-protections)). No date has been announced for changes to keeping permits, so the current Wildlife Regulations 2019 still apply. |
| Venomous species | Specialist permit, in three grades. You normally start at Grade 1 and need 12 months at a grade before applying for the next. Grade 1 includes the whip snakes, curl snake and Red-bellied Black. Grade 2 includes most black snakes, death adders, tigers and browns. Grade 3 is taipans and the Rough-scaled Snake. You need safety, emergency, security and transport plans. ([Keep and sell permits](https://www.environment.sa.gov.au/licences-and-permits/wildlife-permits/keep-sell)) |
| Last checked | 8 Nov 2026 |

> **Authority's internal categories (for Rowan only, never app copy):** permit classes are Class 1 (Basic keep and sell), Class 3 (Specialist keep and sell), Class 9 (sell one animal), and Classes 10 and 11 (eggs). The categories are Unprotected, Exempt, Basic and Specialist, under the NPW (Wildlife) Regulations 2019, Schedule 6.
