## Tasmania

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority and contact | Department of Natural Resources and Environment Tasmania (NRE Tas), Wildlife Services. Phone 1300 368 550 (NRE Tas general enquiries). Email Wildlife.Services@nre.tas.gov.au. Post: GPO Box 44, Hobart TAS 7001. Web: [Herpetofauna permits](https://nre.tas.gov.au/wildlife-management/forms-permits-and-fees/herpetofauna-permits). |
| Official keepable-species list | No published list. Only Tasmanian native reptiles and frogs may be kept. Import rules point to *Requirements for Importing and Keeping Wildlife in Tasmania* ([Importing fauna](https://nre.tas.gov.au/wildlife-management/management-of-wildlife/importing-fauna)). |
| Licence names and what each allows | **Herpetofauna permit.** NRE Tas's formal name is the Special Purposes Wildlife Permit for Herpetofauna. It lets you collect and keep Tasmanian reptiles and amphibians, up to **six adults of any allowed species**, and not from reserved land. Holders must follow the Code of Practice for Herpetology. **Buying and selling are prohibited.** Free exchange between permit holders is allowed. |
| Exempt species | Up to six each of the Common Froglet, Brown Tree Frog, Metallic Skink and Spotted Skink can be kept without a permit, if collected outside reserves. ([Herpetofauna in Tasmania](https://nre.tas.gov.au/wildlife-management/living-with-wildlife/reptiles-in-captivity/herpetology-in-tasmania)) |
| Minimum age and guardian rules | Not stated. |
| Cost (fee year) | Free. |
| Processing time | **Unconfirmed** (not stated). |
| Record keeping and returns | Keep detailed records and husbandry notes for captive animals. The permit is renewed every year, with an **annual return** of animals collected or held. Field observations go to the Natural Values Atlas at least every 12 months. The permit runs for up to one year and expires **31 July**. |
| Buying, selling and moving within the state | No buying or selling. Return surplus animals to where they were caught, and never release frogs, eggs or tadpoles into other water bodies. |
| Recent or upcoming changes | None found. |
| Venomous species | No specific rules found on NRE Tas pages. The Herpetofauna permit covers Tasmanian natives in general, and NRE Tas stresses snake security. The education-facility permit excludes snakes. Check with Wildlife Services before keeping any snake. |
| Last checked | 8 Nov 2026 |

**Importing:** private keepers can't bring a pet snake, lizard or amphibian into Tasmania, including when moving there. Restricted species "will be seized at the border and may be euthanised". Only Wildlife Exhibition Licence holders or approved educational display businesses can import reptiles. ([Importing fauna](https://nre.tas.gov.au/wildlife-management/management-of-wildlife/importing-fauna); [Service Tasmania: import permit](https://service.tas.gov.au/services/working-in-tasmania/agriculture-livestock-and-wildlife/apply-for-an-import-permit-for-wildlife))
