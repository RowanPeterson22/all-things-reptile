## Northern Territory

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority and contact | Parks and Wildlife Commission of the NT, Permits Office. Phone **08 8999 4795** (8 am–4 pm weekdays). Email pwpermits@nt.gov.au. Post: PO Box 496, Palmerston NT 0831. Web: [Keeping protected and prohibited wildlife](https://nt.gov.au/environment/animals/wildlife-permits/keeping-protected-prohibited-wildlife). |
| Official keepable-species list | There is no positive list of keepable non-venomous species. The exempt list is [Animals that do not need a permit](https://nt.gov.au/environment/animals/wildlife-permits/animals-that-do-not-need-a-permit) (undated). Venomous species are grouped in the [Guidelines for Keeping Venomous Snakes in the NT](https://environment.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/255076/guidelines_venomous_snakes.pdf) (v1, Feb 2013). |
| Licence names and what each allows | **No permit:** the six exempt species. **Permit to keep protected or prohibited wildlife:** every other native animal. Apply within 7 days of the sales receipt. Buy only from a lawful, permitted source; the permit doesn't cover taking from the wild and can't be transferred with the animal. Australian natives that don't occur in the NT are "prohibited entrants" and need a permit too. **Venomous snake permits:** three risk levels (mildly dangerous, dangerous, highly dangerous). |
| Exempt species | Six reptiles, if lawfully sourced: Central Bearded Dragon, Central Netted Dragon, Centralian Blue-tongue, Common Blue-tongue, Children's Python and Northern Long-necked (snake-necked) Turtle. No frogs. |
| Minimum age and guardian rules | Not stated on the permit pages. For venomous snakes, the 2013 guidelines require the permit holder to be 18+, and 14–17-year-olds may be a nominee on a parent's permit. That is from the guidelines, not current web pages. |
| Cost (fee year) | **Unconfirmed.** No fee is published. ABLIS says to contact the agency. |
| Processing time | At least 5 working days. Venomous: about 5 days for mildly dangerous, 2 weeks for dangerous and highly dangerous. |
| Record keeping and returns | Renew by lodging a "Return notice for protected wildlife and prohibited entrants" by the date on your notice. The page still shows 15 Feb 2024, which is out of date. Other record-keeping rules are **unconfirmed**. |
| Buying, selling and moving within the state | Buy only from a person, shop or breeder with a current permit to keep and trade. Interstate import or export needs a separate permit. |
| Recent or upcoming changes | None found. Several NT pages haven't been updated in years (the snake guide is dated 12 Jan 2016). |
| Venomous species | Permits by risk level, with a Parks and Wildlife property inspection. Council, Health or Lands, Planning and Environment approvals may also be needed. No permits are issued for copperheads, Broad-headed, Tiger, Rough-scaled or Eastern Small-eyed snakes, or for any exotic snake. |
| Last checked | 8 Nov 2026 |

> **Authority's internal categories (for Rowan only, never app copy):** venomous snakes are Category 1 (mildly dangerous), Category 2 (dangerous) and Category 3 (highly dangerous). Wildlife status is "protected" (NT natives) or "prohibited entrant" (non-NT species), under the TPWC Act 1976.
