## Interstate movement, federal rules and change log

### Interstate movement

Moving a reptile or frog across a border almost always needs **two approvals**: an export approval from the state it leaves and an import approval from the state it enters. Both keepers must also be licensed for the species. A licence in your home state doesn't mean the destination will accept the animal. Get both approvals before the animal moves.

### Federal rules

- **No live reptile imports as pets.** The federal government says reptiles "are not permitted to be imported as pets"; zoological imports are possible only under strict conditions. Frogs and other amphibians come in only for laboratory or zoological purposes. ([Unique or exotic pets](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/travelling/bringing-mailing-goods/unique-exotic-pets), last reviewed 4 Feb 2020; checked 8 Nov 2026.) Exotic reptiles already in Australia can't legally be kept by private keepers in any state or territory.
- **Live Import List.** Only species on the Live Import List under the *EPBC Act 1999* (Part 13A) can be imported live. Anything not on it is illegal to import, and DCCEEW treats possessing, breeding or trading such species as an offence. The list is published by DCCEEW at dcceew.gov.au/environment/wildlife-trade/live-import-list. That page couldn't be fetched in this check, so this point is **unconfirmed on the official page** and comes from secondary reports that link to it.
- **Dubia roaches and other illegal feeders.** Dubia roaches (*Blaptica dubia*) and Madagascar Hissing Cockroaches aren't on the Live Import List. In June 2026 DCCEEW announced the seizure of more than 100,000 live exotic cockroaches from a commercial breeder in Bathurst, NSW, and warned that exotic cockroaches can't legally be kept, bred or sold. ([Mongabay, 15 Jun 2026](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/06/australian-authorities-seize-100000-live-cockroaches-in-crackdown-on-exotic-insect-trade/); [The New Daily, 5 Jun 2026](https://thenewdaily.com.au/news/national/2026/06/05/illegal-cockroaches-seized). Both are secondary sources quoting DCCEEW. The official release at dcceew.gov.au/about/news/massive-seizure-illegal-exotic-cockroaches-prompts-warning blocks automated fetching and is **unconfirmed** by direct read.) Use Australian Wood Roaches; BSFL are sold as Calci-worms.
- **Exporting native reptiles overseas** is banned for private keepers under federal law (EPBC Act Part 13A; NRE Tas also states this). CITES matters to keepers only through this export ban and the import ban above.
